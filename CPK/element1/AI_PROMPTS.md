## 🚀 Pro Tips

1. **Use specific element symbols** instead of names for faster processing
   - ✅ "Show Au" instead of "Show Gold"
   - ✅ "Filter to Fe, Co, Ni" instead of "Filter to Iron, Cobalt, Nickel"

2. **Combine filter + visualization** for powerful analysis
   - "Filter to Group 1, highlight by ionization energy, create bar chart"

3. **Ask for specific statistics**
   - Instead of "Analyze Period 2" → "Get mean atomic mass of Period 2"

4. **Use the UI features while chatting**
   - Click elements while asking questions about them
   - This helps the AI understand your focus

5. **Ask for comparisons between categories**
   - "Compare average density: metals vs nonmetals"
